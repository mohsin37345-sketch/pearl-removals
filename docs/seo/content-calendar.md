## Editorial Guidelines for Content Creation

1. **Tone:** Authoritative, reassuring, articulate UK English. Plain-speaking London professional.
2. **Word Count:** 1,000–1,500 words per blog article.
3. **Structured Data:** Every article must include `BlogPosting` or `Article` schema with author, date, and `FAQPage` schema if FAQs are included.
4. **Internal Linking Rules:**
   - Every post links to at least **2 core or specialist services** using descriptive anchor text.
   - Every post links to at least **1 target location page** or micro-area (Phase 1 or Phase 2).
5. **No AI Fluff:** Include concrete council names, bus routes, station names, and realistic logistical steps.
