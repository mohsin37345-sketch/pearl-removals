## 2. Local Press Digital PR & Newsjack Angles

Targeted outreach to regional London publications: **Ham & High (Hampstead & Highgate Express)**, **Brent & Kilburn Times**, **Camden New Journal**, and **Barnet Borough Times**.

### Campaign 1: "The North West London Moving Costs Index" (Annual Data Study)
- **Concept:** Pearl Removals releases proprietary research analyzing 1,000+ local London property moves, revealing average moving day costs, parking suspension fees by borough (comparing Camden vs Brent vs Barnet), and the busiest moving dates in London.
- **Hook:** "Why Camden residents pay up to 40% more in parking suspensions on moving day than neighbouring Barnet."
- **Asset:** Downloadable PDF report and interactive summary page on the website.
- **Distribution:** Exclusive pitch to property editors at Ham & High and the Evening Standard Homes & Property section.

### Campaign 2: "Heritage Property Moving Challenges in Hampstead & Highgate"
- **Concept:** Feature editorial explaining the logistical marvel of moving grand pianos, fine art, and heirlooms through Grade II listed cottages, narrow carriage lanes, and steep conservation paths.
- **Hook:** Expert tips from our operations director on how to avoid structural damage to period staircases.

### Campaign 3: "Downsizing from London Mansions: Trends Among Retirees"
- **Concept:** Op-ed discussing the trend of long-term homeowners in Mill Hill, Hampstead Garden Suburb, and St John's Wood downsizing to lateral apartments in central London and the home counties.

## 6. Prohibited Link Practices (Strict Enforcement)

To protect domain integrity and ensure compliance with Google's Spam Policies:
- ❌ **No Private Blog Networks (PBNs)** or expired domain redirects.
- ❌ **No Fiverr or low-cost bulk link packages** (e.g. "1,000 forum backlinks").
- ❌ **No automated comment or web 2.0 spam**.
- ❌ **No paid dofollow sponsored posts** without `rel="sponsored"` or `rel="nofollow"`.
- ❌ **No exact-match anchor text over-optimization**; maintain a healthy ratio of branded ("Pearl Removals"), URL ("pearlremovals.co.uk"), and natural anchors ("visit website", "removal company").
