### 5. Hendon & West Hendon: Family Houses, Reservoir Walks & Transit Links

Hendon provides exceptional value for growing families seeking four-bedroom 1930s semis, expansive gardens, and proximity to the open waters of the Welsh Harp (Brent Reservoir). With Thameslink services reaching St Pancras in under 15 minutes, Hendon Central on the Northern line, and rapid road links to the M1 and A406 at Staples Corner, commuter flexibility is unmatched. Families benefit from St Joseph's and Hendon Prep schools alongside verdant recreational grounds.
